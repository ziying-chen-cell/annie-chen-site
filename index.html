02

TikTok — Global Product Solutions & Operations

Proving carousel ads outperform video, at global scale

Context

The Global Product Solutions & Operations team needed a data-backed answer to a recurring client question: which ad format actually converts — video or carousel — and where should product and sales teams focus next.

Approach

I designed and ran an A/B test spanning 30+ global campaigns, queried performance data with SQL, and analyzed CTR, CVR, CPA, and ROAS in Python to isolate format performance by vertical.

Impact

Carousel ads beat video by 18% in CVR. The finding shaped product positioning and go-to-market rollout, and I partnered with Sales and Client teams to turn it into 8 documented case studies and SOPs that drove 5%+ feature adoption.

A/B TestingSQLPythonGo-to-Market
03

Ipsos — Marketing Understanding & Innovation

Turning 50,000+ survey responses into a launch strategy for Unilever

Context

Three global FMCG clients needed segmentation and brand-performance insight they could act on quickly — including Unilever, mid-way through validating a new shampoo concept.

Approach

I ran SQL extractions and SPSS statistical analysis across 50,000+ consumer responses, then built a structured framework spanning awareness, purchase intent, and usage to lead end-to-end concept and product validation.

Impact

Delivered segmentation and brand insight that cut turnaround time by ~25%, and the validation framework directly shaped Unilever's advertising, merchandising, and go-to-market strategy.

04

Personal Project — LangChain / Claude API / Streamlit

Building an AI analyst that answers business questions in plain English

Context

Marketing and business teams often have the questions but not the SQL — I wanted to close that gap with a tool that turns plain-language questions into real analysis.

Approach

I built a multi-tool LangChain ReAct agent — SQL query module, Python executor, and Matplotlib visualization pipeline — on the Claude API, with a Streamlit interface supporting CSV/database upload and real-time analytics.

Impact

Reached 89% intent accuracy across anomaly detection, forecasting, and YoY/MoM analysis — and using Claude Code, I compressed an estimated two-week build into three days.
